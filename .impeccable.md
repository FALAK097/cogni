# Design Context

## Audience

Small SaaS teams that need to answer customer questions with an AI agent and a shared human support inbox.

## Core jobs

- Build, test, and deploy a grounded AI support agent.
- Review and respond to customer conversations with teammates.
- Understand what is working and what needs attention without navigating a maze of pages.

## Brand personality

Calm, clear, reliable.

## Design principles

- Keep the primary navigation focused on Inbox, Agent, and Insights, with workspace settings secondary.
- Follow Cogni's existing design tokens and component patterns; use competitor products as interaction references, not as a source for copied branding or unverified claims.
- Prefer clear hierarchy, concise language, accessible controls, responsive layouts, and progressive disclosure over extra pages or decorative UI.
- Show honest data states and make the next useful action obvious.
