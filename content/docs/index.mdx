Cogni brings an AI agent, grounded knowledge, an embeddable website widget, and a shared conversation inbox into one workspace.

## Product workflow

1. **Configure the agent.** Choose its model, instructions, behavior, and handoff rules.
2. **Add knowledge.** Index websites, sitemaps, documents, or pasted text for grounded answers.
3. **Test before deployment.** Exercise representative questions and inspect answers, sources, and handoff behavior.
4. **Publish and install.** Publish a version, allow the production domain, and add the widget loader to the site.
5. **Operate conversations.** Review activity in the inbox, take over when needed, and measure performance in Analytics.

Start with [Getting started](/docs/getting-started), or open the guide for the area you are configuring.
