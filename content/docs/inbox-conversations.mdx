---
title: Inbox, conversations, and human takeover
description: Triage customer conversations, collaborate with teammates, and control when the AI participates.
---

The inbox combines website and connected-channel conversations for the active workspace.

## Find and organize conversations

Use search, channel, assignee, and label filters to narrow the list. Built-in views include unread, unassigned, mine, open, closed, snoozed, and tickets. Saved views preserve a useful filter combination for repeated workflows.

Opening a conversation shows its public messages, internal activity, sources, attachments, contact context, and previous conversations when available.

## Reply and collaborate

Choose a public reply to answer the visitor or an internal note to collaborate without sending a customer-visible message. Saved replies and macros can speed up repeated responses.

Assign the conversation to a teammate, add labels and contact tags, snooze work until later, or create a ticket with status, priority, assignee, and due date.

## Human takeover

A visitor can request a person, or the agent can emit handoff evidence based on its configured keywords and behavior. The conversation then enters the human workflow.

When a teammate takes over, Cogni assigns the conversation and pauses AI replies. The teammate can send public replies while the AI remains paused. Resume AI only after the human-led exchange is complete and the conversation is suitable for automation again.

Assignment and AI state are separate: changing the assignee does not by itself resume the agent.

## Conversation status

Use status to reflect the current work state. Close a conversation when the exchange is complete; snooze it when action should resume later. These states also feed inbox filters and analytics.
