title: Analytics
description: Interpret conversation, engagement, response, satisfaction, and knowledge-gap metrics.
---

Analytics reports on conversations in the active workspace and selected date range. Comparative indicators use the preceding range of equal length.

## Core metrics

- **Total conversations** counts conversations started in the selected range.
- **Visitor sessions** counts the visitor sessions represented by those conversations.
- **Closed now** counts selected-range conversations whose current status is closed.
- **Average AI response time** measures from a visitor message to the next public AI reply. Human-only and unanswered turns are excluded.
- **Satisfaction** maps positive feedback to 5 and negative feedback to 0, then averages recorded feedback.

Charts break activity down over time, source, and current status. Engagement reporting includes messages sent and received, engagement rate, conversations per user, and satisfaction over time.

## Questions and knowledge gaps

Top questions show recurring customer demand. Knowledge gaps identify signals such as unanswered questions or responses without a supporting source.

Review a gap in its conversation context, then improve the relevant knowledge source or create a repeatable agent test. Gaps can be tracked as open, resolved, or ignored without deleting the underlying conversation evidence.

## Conversation quality

Conversation quality insights score helpfulness and grounding from the recorded exchange. Use them as review signals, not as a substitute for reading the conversation and checking the cited knowledge.

## Export

Use the available export control to take the current analytics view into a file for further analysis. The export follows the active workspace and date-range context.
